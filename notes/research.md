### What is it
The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method that uses timeboxing. It is a collection of  iterative loops aimed to help a person focus on specific tasks.

### Goal
The goal of the pomodoro loop is for the participant to have a way to plan, track, record, process, and visualize progress towards a goal.

### Pomodoro Loop
A pomodoro loop is a collection of activity loops. 3 activity loops and 1 long break make up 1 pomodoro loop.

Once the participant has accomplished this, it is considered one pomodoro.

#### Pomodoro Loop
3 x activity loops
1 x long break

### Activity Loop
An activity loop is a collection of one task interval and one break.

A task interval is a set block of time in which the participant is focused solely on a task without interuption or deviation.

A break interval, also known as a short break, is a set block of time in which the participant is focused on anything except the activity being accomplished in the task interval. The break is set to a specific time without interuption or deviation.

### Time Intervals
A time interval is the amount of time that passes between the start and the end. This can also be referred to as elapsed time or a duration of time. In the case of a pomodoro, this a predefined set amount of time that cannot be changed by the participant during the pomodoro loop.

### Starting The Loop
Before the participant starts to do a pomodoro loop, they must set 3 specific time intervals.

task: a goal in which to accomplish within a set of time
task interval: must be a preselected time (default: 25 minutes)
short break interval: must be a preselected time (default: 5 minutes)
long break: must be preselected (default: 30 minutes)

### Exiting The Loop
A participant can only break out of the loop in one of 2 ways:

1. The participant an exit the loop iff they have completed one pomodoro.
2. The participant an exit the loop iff they have accomplished the task during a 
task interval and have felt they have completed the during a short break
3. There is an overriding emergency in which they must exit the loop.

### Norms and Variations

Under the traditional pomodoro technique, the activity loop's time interval is 25 minutes and the break is 5 minutes. After 3 activity loops have been ran, the long break is 30 minutes. 

### Example:

Participant decides to read a book in 1 day. 

Participant decides each time interval is a chapter in a book. There are 6 chapters in the book and it takes 25 minutes to read a chapter.

They enter the pomodoro loop:

25 minutes - active focus on chapter 1
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
25 minutes - active focus on chapter 2
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
25 minutes - active focus on chapter 3
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
30 minutes - lunch (first pomodoro complete)
25 minutes - active focus on chapter 4
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
25 minutes - active focus on chapter 5
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
25 minutes - active focus on chapter 6
5 minutes - rest/select a different album to listen to
30 minutes - coffee and finalizes notes (second pomodoro complete)
